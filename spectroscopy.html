Thermal Spectroscopy Research

Experimental measurement of thermal wave propagation in materials using high-powered lasers and precision optical equipment.

Experimental Testing • Manufacturing • Heat Transfer • Data Collection

Project Overview

I worked as an undergraduate research assistant on an experimental thermal spectroscopy system used to investigate thermal wave propagation and thermal diffusivity in material samples.

My work focused on manufacturing and assembling the physical structures supporting the experimental equipment, operating the completed optical system during testing, and collecting measurements using sensitive instrumentation.

Experimental Setup & Manufacturing

I manufactured and assembled supporting components used to position and secure the experimental equipment. This included building the physical structure surrounding the optical instrumentation and ensuring that the equipment could be securely integrated into the experimental setup.

The optical components, including the mirrors and laser alignment, were positioned by other researchers. My role focused on the mechanical support hardware and physical construction of the setup.

Laser Testing & Data Collection

I operated the experimental system during testing in a controlled dark-room environment using high-powered lasers and highly sensitive measurement equipment.

Measurements captured the response of thermal waves traveling through the material samples. Because the experiment relied on precise optical measurements, testing required careful operation of the equipment and consistent experimental conditions.

I processed the collected measurements to perform an initial evaluation of thermal diffusivity across the tested materials. This provided experience working with experimental heat-transfer data from acquisition through preliminary analysis.

Sample Preparation

I also performed calculations related to the thin gold film applied to material samples. The coating was used to improve the samples' thermal and optical behavior during laser measurements.

These calculations were used to estimate an appropriate amount of gold coating for the samples while considering its effect on the experiment.
